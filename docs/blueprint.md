# **App Name**: RecipeSage

## Core Features:

- Recipe Generator: AI-powered recipe generation: Generates full recipes (ingredients and instructions) based on the dish name.
- Recipe Contextualizer: Enhance user experience with the help of AI that acts as a 'tool' to determine and insert the recipe's country of origin to generate an additional element of background/branding of the dish
- Recipe Display: Clear, structured display of generated recipes, with ingredients list and step-by-step instructions
- Recipe Rating: Users can like/dislike the recipes generated in order to have a personal reference to what they tried/liked/did not like.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy orange (#C45A00), evoking warmth and appetite.
- Background color: Light beige (#F2E8D0), a neutral backdrop to showcase recipes.
- Accent color: Muted olive green (#748E54), complementing the orange for a natural feel.
- Font pairing: 'Playfair' (serif) for headlines, 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text
- Use line icons related to food preparation.
- Clean, card-based design with generous spacing.
- Subtle transitions on recipe loading.
