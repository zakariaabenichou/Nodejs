Chocolate And Coconut Pudding

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + chill time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup coconut milk
  • 2.0 tbsp cacao powder or organic cocoa
  • 0.5 tsp Stevia powder extract or 2 tbsp honey/maple syrup
  • 0.5 tbsp quality gelatin
  • 1.0 tbsp water

Instructions

  1. On medium heat, combine the coconut milk, cocoa, and sweetener.
  2. In a separate bowl, mix in the gelatin and water
  3. Add to the pan and stir until fully dissolved.
  4. Pour into small dishes and refrigerate for 1 hour.
  5. Serve!
