A Broccoli And Tilapia Dish To Die For!

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 4.0 mins –

Cook Time: 14.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 6.0 ounce of tilapia, frozen
  • 1.0 tablespoon of butter
  • 1.0 tablespoon of garlic, minced
  • 1.0 teaspoon of lemon pepper seasoning
  • 1.0 cup of broccoli florets, fresh

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degree Fahrenheit
  2. Add fish in aluminum foil packets
  3. Arrange broccoli around fish
  4. Sprinkle lemon pepper on top
  5. Close the packets and seal
  6. Bake for 14 minutes
  7. Take a bowl and add garlic and butter, mix well and keep the mixture on the side
  8. Remove the packet from oven and transfer to platter
  9. Place butter on top of the fish and broccoli, serve and enjoy!
