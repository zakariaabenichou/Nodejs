Exotic Reishi Pear

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup ice
  • 1.0 cup collard greens, chopped
  • 1.0 pear, roughly chopped
  • 0.25 cup raw cashews
  • 1.0 teaspoon reishi mushroom powder
  • 1.0 cup unsweetened cashew milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
