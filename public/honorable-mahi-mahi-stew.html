Honorable Mahi Mahi Stew

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tablespoons butter
  • 0.75 pound cubed Mahi Mahi fillets
  • 0.5 chopped onion
  • Salt & Black pepper
  • 0.75 cup homemade fish broth

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle the Mahi Mahi fillets with some seasonings.
  2. Put the butter in a pressure cooker to melt, then add the onions
  3. Cook the onions for 3 minutes, then add the fish broth and mahi-mahi fillets
  4. Cook for 30 minutes with the lid sealed at high pressure
  5. Release the pressure naturally.
  6. Serve!
