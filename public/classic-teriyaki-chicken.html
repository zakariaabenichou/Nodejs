Classic Teriyaki Chicken

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 50.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 chicken thighs, boneless
  • 2.0 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1.0 tbsp swerve sweetener
  • 1.0 tbsp avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Take a skillet pan, place it over medium heat, add oil and when hot, add chicken thighs and cook for 5 minutes per side until seared.
  2. Then sprinkle sugar over chicken thighs, drizzle with soy sauce and bring the sauce to boil.
  3. Switch heat to medium-low level, continue cooking for 3 minutes until chicken is evenly glazed, and then transfer to a plate.
  4. Serve chicken with cauliflower rice.
