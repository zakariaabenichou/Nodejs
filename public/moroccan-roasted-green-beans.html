Moroccan Roasted Green Beans

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 5.0 oz green beans 1/2 tsp salt
  • 0.5 tbsp Moroccan spice mix
  • 1.5 tbsp avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Turn on the oven, then set it to 400 degrees F and let it preheat.
  2. Take a medium bowl, place green beans in it, add oil, salt, spice mix and toss until well coated. Spread green beans on a sheet pan and then bake for 15 minutes until roasted, stirring halfway. Serve.
