Elegant Pumpkin Chili Dish

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 cups yellow onion, chopped
  • 8.0 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1.0 pound turkey, ground
  • 2.0 cans (15 ounces each) fire roasted tomatoes
  • 2.0 cups pumpkin puree
  • 1.0 cup chicken broth
  • 4.0 teaspoons chili spice
  • 1.0 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1.0 teaspoon sea salt

Instructions

  1. Take a large sized pot and place it over medium-high heat
  2. Add coconut oil and let the oil heat up
  3. Add onion and garlic, Saute for 5 minutes
  4. Add ground turkey and break it while cooking, cook for 5 minutes
  5. Add remaining ingredients and bring the mix to simmer
  6. Simmer for 15 minutes over low heat (lid off)
  7. Pour chicken broth
  8. Serve with desired salad
  9. Enjoy!
