Creative Lamb Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 35.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup olive oil
  • 0.25 cup mint, fresh and chopped
  • 8.0 lamb rib chops
  • 1.0 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1.0 tablespoon rosemary, fresh and chopped

Instructions

  1. Add rosemary, garlic, mint, olive oil into a bowl and mix well
  2. Keep a tablespoon of the mixture on the side for later use
  3. Toss lamb chops into the marinade, letting them marinate for 30 minutes
  4. Take a cast iron skillet and place it over medium-high heat
  5. Add lamb and cook for 2 minutes per side for medium rare
  6. Let the lamb rest for a few minutes and drizzle remaining
  7. Serve and enjoy!
