Colorful Vegetable Omelet

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1.0 cup Chanterelle mushrooms, chopped
  • 2.0 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1.0 white onion, chopped
  • 6.0 eggs

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over moderate heat. Now, cook the mushrooms, peppers, and onion until they have softened.
  2. In a mixing bowl, whisk the eggs until frothy. Add the eggs to the skillet, reduce the heat to medium-low, and cook for approximately 5 minutes until the center starts to look dry. Do not overcook.
  3. Taste and season with salt to taste
