Raspberry Pudding Meal

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + Chill Time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 tbsp chia seeds
  • 0.5 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1.0 scoop chocolate protein powder
  • 0.25 cup raspberries, fresh or frozen

Instructions

  1. Combine the almond milk, protein powder, and chia seeds together
