Onion And Bacon Pork Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 45.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 onions, peeled and chopped
  • 6.0 bacon slices, chopped
  • 0.5 cup chicken stock
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4.0 pork chops

Instructions

  1. Heat up pan over medium-heat and add bacon
  2. Stir and cook until crispy; Transfer to bowl
  3. Return pan to medium heat and add onions, season with salt and pepper
  4. Stir and cook for 15 minutes; Transfer to same bowl with bacon
  5. Return the pan to heat (medium-high) and add pork chops
  6. Season with salt and pepper and brown for 3 minutes
  7. Flip and lower heat to medium
  8. Cook for 7 minutes more
  9. Add stock and stir cook for 2 minutes
  10. Return the bacon and onions to the pan and stir cook for 1 minute; Serve and enjoy!

  • Calories: 325.0 kcal
  • Fat: 18.0g
