Garlic Touched Beans

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1.0 cup butter
  • 2.0 cloves garlic, minced 1 cup toasted pine nuts

Instructions

  1. Boil a pot of water.
  2. Boil a pot of water.
  3. Heat the butter in a large skillet over high heat. Add the garlic and pine nuts and sauté for 2 minutes or until the pine nuts are lightly browned.
  4. Transfer the green beans to the skillet and turn until coated
  5. Serve!
