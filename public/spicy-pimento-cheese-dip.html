Spicy Pimento Cheese Dip

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 10.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 brick cream cheese
  • 10.0 cherry peppers, chopped
  • 1.0 cups cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1.5 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1.0 Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat up garlic in a pan over medium heat
  2. Drop cream cheese and let it soft, stir consistently
  3. Mix in cheddar, add chopped pepper
  4. Stir and enjoy with your desired dippers!
