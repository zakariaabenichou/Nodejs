Beautiful Tarragon Chicken Roast

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 45.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2.0 tbsp of fresh tarragon leaves, chopped
  • 3.0 shallots, thoroughly chopped
  • 1.0 tbsp of garlic salt
  • 2.0 tbsp of olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 380F/180 C.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the olive oil, garlic salt, and tarragon with the shallots.
  3. Brush the mixture over the chicken pieces.
  4. Bake in a greased baking dish for 40 minutes and switch on the broiler for the last five minutes to form a light crust.
  5. Serve with salad or cauliflower rice
