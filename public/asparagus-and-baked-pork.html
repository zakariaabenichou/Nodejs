Asparagus And Baked Pork

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound (454 g) asparagus, tough ends removed
  • 0.5 cup roughly ground pork rinds
  • 1.0 cup ranch dressing Pinch of sea sal

Instructions

  1. Arrange the asparagus spears in a casserole dish. Spread the pork rinds and ranch dressing over the asparagus, then season with sea salt.
  2. Place the casserole dish in the preheated oven and bake for 18 minutes or until lightly browned.
  3. Transfer them onto a platter and serve warm.
