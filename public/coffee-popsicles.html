Coffee Popsicles

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons chocolate chips, sugar-free
  • 2.0 cups coffee, brewed and cold
  • 0.75 cup heavy whip cream
  • 2.0 teaspoons natural sweetener

Instructions

  1. Blend in heavy whip cream, sweetened, and coffee in your blender.
  2. Mix them well.
  3. Pour the mix into popsicle molds.
  4. Add a few chocolate chips.
  5. Keep in the fridge for 2 hours.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
