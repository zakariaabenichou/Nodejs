Italian Pork Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 pork chops
  • 1.0 tablespoons fresh oregano, chopped
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
  • 1.0 tablespoon canola oil
  • 15.0 ounces canned tomatoes, diced
  • 1.0 tablespoon tomato paste
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 0.25 cup tomato juice

Instructions

  1. Heat up pan with oil over medium-high heat
  2. Add pork chops, season with salt and pepper
  3. Cook for 3 minutes; Flip and cook for 3 minutes more
  4. Return the pan to medium heat
  5. Add garlic and stir cook for 10 seconds
  6. Add tomato juice, tomato paste and tomatoes, bring to a boil and lower heat to medium-low
  7. Add pork chops , stir and cover pan
  8. Simmer for 30 minutes
  9. Transfer chops to platter and add oregano to pan, stir cook for 2 minutes
  10. Pour over pork chops; Serve and enjoy!
