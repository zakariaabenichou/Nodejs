The Duck Eye Ribeye

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 16-oz ribeye steak (1 - 1 114 inches thick)
  • 1.0 tbsp duck fat (or other high smoke point oil like peanut oil)
  • 0.5 tbsp butter
  • 0.5 tsp thyme, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat a skillet in your oven at 400°F/200°C
  2. Season the steaks with oil, salt, and pepper. Remove the skillet from the oven once pre-heated.
  3. Put the skillet on your stove top burner on medium heat and drizzle in the oil.
  4. Sear the steak for 1-4 minutes, depending on if you like it rare, medium, or well done.
  5. Turn over the steak and place it in your oven for 6 minutes.
  6. Take out the steak from your oven and place it back on the stove top on low heat.
  7. Toss in the butter and thyme and cook for 3 minutes, basting as you go along.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes and serve
