Stir Fried Zucchini And Green Beans

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 tablespoons olive oil
  • 0.5 small zucchini, thinly sliced
  • 0.5 cup green beans, cut into small pieces
  • Sea salt, to taste
  • 2.0 tablespoons scallions, chopped
  • 2.0 tablespoons lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add and stir fry the zucchini, green beans, and salt for 9 minutes or until soft and crisp.
  3. Remove them from the skillet to a plate. Garnish with chopped scallion and serve drizzled with lemon juice.
