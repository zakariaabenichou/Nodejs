Morning Aloha Smoothie

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

  • 1.0 cup ice
  • 1.0 tablespoon coconut, shredded
  • 1.0 cup of frozen mango, sliced
  • 0.75 cup pineapple sliced
  • 1.25 cups unsweetened almond milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
