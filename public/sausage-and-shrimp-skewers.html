Sausage And Shrimp Skewers

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 20.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 large smoked sausages cut into 30-35 slices in total
  • 2.0 medium-sized zucchinis, cut into 35-40 slices
  • 40.0 shrimps, peeled with tail on
  • 1.0 batch of low carb barbeque sauce

Instructions

  1. Take each skewer and thread 2 pieces of shrimp, two zucchini, and two sausages (one ingredient, then another to make a pattern).
  2. Brush the barbeque sauce over the shrimp and zucchini and cook over a grill pan for 3-4 minutes on each side.
  3. Serve optionally with chopped lettuce and some extra barbeque sauce

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 178.0 kcal
  • Fat: 12.0g
  • Carbs: 3.0g
  • Protein: 12.0g
