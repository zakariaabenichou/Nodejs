The Most Elegant Parsley Soufflé Ever

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 6.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 5.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 whole eggs
  • 1.0 fresh red chili pepper, chopped
  • 2.0 tablespoons coconut cream
  • 1.0 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 390 degree F
  2. Butter 2 soufflé dishes
  3. Add the ingredients to a blender and mix well
  4. Divide batter into soufflé dishes and bake for 6 minutes
  5. Serve and enjoy!
