Flax And Kiwi Spinach Smoothie

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup crushed ice
  • 3.0 tablespoons ground flax
  • 3.0 kiwis, diced
  • 1.0 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1.0 banana, chopped
  • 2.0 apples, quartered
  • 1.0 cup spinach, chopped

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
