The Ultimate Morning Green

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 medium whole tomato, diced
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach, chopped
  • 1.0 medium sweet green pepper, chopped
  • 15.0 green grapes
  • 1.0 cucumber, cubed
  • 2.0 medium carrots, scrubbed and chopped
  • 2.0 medium Granny Smith apples, quartered

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
