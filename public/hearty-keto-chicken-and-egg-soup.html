Hearty Keto Chicken And Egg Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup chicken broth
  • 2.0 whole eggs
  • 1.0 teaspoon chili garlic paste
  • 1.0 tablespoon bacon grease
  • 0.5 a cube, chicken bouillon

Instructions

  1. Take a stove top pan and place it over medium-high heat
  2. Add chicken broth, bouillon cube, bacon grease and stir
  3. Bring the mix to a boil
  4. Mix in chili garlic paste
  5. Take a bowl and whisk in eggs, add whisked egg to the pan
  6. Lower down heat and gently simmer for a few minutes
  7. Serve and enjoy!
