Walnuts And Asparagus Delight

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tablespoons olive oil
  • 0.75 pound asparagus, trimmed
  • 0.25 cup walnuts, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Place a skillet over medium heat add olive oil and let it heat up
  2. Add asparagus, Saute for 5 minutes until browned
  3. Season with salt and pepper
  4. Remove heat
  5. Add walnuts and toss
  6. Serve warm!

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 124.0 kcal
  • Fat: 12.0g
  • Carbs: 2.0g
  • Protein: 3.0g
