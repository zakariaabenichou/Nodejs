Pink Yogurt Popsicles

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 3-5 hours chill time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 ounces frozen mango, diced
  • 8.0 ounces frozen strawberries
  • 1.0 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2.5 teaspoons heavy whip cream
  • 1.0 teaspoon vanilla essence

Instructions

  1. Blend the listed ingredients into your blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour the mix into popsicle molds.
  4. Keep in the fridge for 3-5 hours.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
