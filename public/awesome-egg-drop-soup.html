Awesome Egg Drop Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup chicken breast, cooked, boneless, and skinless
  • 1.0 egg, lightly beaten
  • 0.5 teaspoon coconut aminos + 4 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • 0.5 cup green beans, frozen
  • 0.25 cup green onion, sliced

Instructions

  1. Add chicken stock and soy sauce to a saucepan.
  2. Place it over medium heat.
  3. Bring the mix to a boil and add green onions, peas, and chicken.
  4. Stir it and bring the mix to boil once again.
  5. Remove the heat and slowly drizzle in the egg.
  6. Set it aside for a few minutes.
  7. Stir and transfer into a serving bowl, then ladle the soup
  8. Serve and enjoy!
