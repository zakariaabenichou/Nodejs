Everyone’s Favorite Turkey Avocado Rolls

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 slices (12 oz) turkey breast
  • 12.0 slices Swiss cheese
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach
  • 1.0 large avocado, cut into
  • 12.0 slices
  • 1.0 cup homemade mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Lay out the slices of turkey breast flat and place a slice of Swiss cheese on top of each one.
  2. Top each slice with 1 cup baby spinach and 3 slices of avocado.
  3. Drizzle the mayonnaise on top
  4. Sprinkle each "sandwich" with lemon pepper.
  5. Roll up the sandwiches and secure them with toothpicks.
  6. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve.
