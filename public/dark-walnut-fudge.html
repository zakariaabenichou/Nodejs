Dark Walnut Fudge

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.5 oz butter, soft
  • 4.5 oz of cream cheese, soft
  • 3.0 tbsp dark cocoa powder
  • 1.0 tsp vanilla + 2 tbsp granulated sweetener
  • 1.5 oz of walnut pieces

Instructions

  1. Take a bowl and mix in all ingredients
  2. Transfer the mixture to a lined dish
  3. Transfer the dish to your fridge and let it chill for 2-3 hours
  4. Slice and serve
  5. Enjoy!
