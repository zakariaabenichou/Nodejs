Crazy Beef Meatballs

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 lb ground beef
  • 0.5 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1.0 tbsp minced garlic (or paste)
  • 0.5 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1.0 tsp freshly ground pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F/200°C.
  2. In a bowl, mix all the ingredients together.
  3. Roll the meat mixture into 6 generous meatballs.
  4. Bake inside your oven at 170°F/80°C for about 18 minutes.
  5. Serve with sauce!
