Awesome Pineapple And Carrot Blend

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.125 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1.0 cup fresh pineapple
  • 3.0 organic carrots, scrubbed and sliced
  • 5.0 cups baby spinach
  • 1.0 large cucumber, diced

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
