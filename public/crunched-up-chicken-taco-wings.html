Crunched Up Chicken Taco Wings

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 pounds (1.4 kg) chicken wings
  • 1.0 tablespoon taco seasoning mix
  • 2.0 teaspoons olive oil

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken wings in a Ziploc bag, then add the taco seasoning and olive oil.
  2. Seal the bag and shake well until the chicken is coated thoroughly
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 350°F (180°C).
  4. Put the chicken in the air fryer basket and cook for 6 minutes on each side until crispy
  5. Remove the chicken from the basket and serve on a plate
