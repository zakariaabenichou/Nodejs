Fine Spinach And Cheese Eggs

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 hole eggs
  • 3.0 oz cottage cheese
  • 4.0 oz chopped spinach 1/4 cup parmesan cheese 1/4 cup of milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F/190°C.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, cottage cheese, parmesan, and milk.
  3. Mix in the spinach.
  4. Transfer to a small, greased oven dish.
  5. Sprinkle the cheese on top.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes.
  7. Let cool for 5 minutes and serve.
  8. Serve!

  • Calories: 200.0 kcal
  • Fat: 25.0g
  • Carbs: 2.0g
  • Protein: 16.0g
