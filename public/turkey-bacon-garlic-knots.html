Turkey Bacon Garlic Knots

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 slices of turkey bacon
  • 1.0 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.25 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 0.25 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2.0 tbsp grated parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Turn on the oven, then set it to 425 degrees F, and let preheat.
  2. Working on a slice of bacon at a time, tie it into a double knot, and then place it on a baking sheet.
  3. Prepare remaining bacon knots in the same manner, season evenly with garlic powder, red pepper, and Italian seasoning, and then bake for 7 to 10 minutes until almost crisp.
  4. Then sprinkle cheese over bacon knots and continue baking for 3 to 4 minutes until cheese has melted.
  5. Serve.
