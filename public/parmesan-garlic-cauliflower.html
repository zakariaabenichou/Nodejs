Parmesan Garlic Cauliflower

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 50.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.75 cup cauliflower florets 2 tbsp butter
  • 1.0 clove garlic, sliced thinly
  • 2.0 tbsp shredded parmesan 1 pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F/175°C.
  2. On low heat, melt the butter with the garlic for 5-10 minutes.
  3. Strain the garlic in a sieve.
  4. Add the cauliflower, parmesan, and salt
  5. Bake for 20 minutes or until golden.
  6. Serve and enjoy once done!
