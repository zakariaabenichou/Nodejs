The Classical Medi Pork

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 pork chops, bone-in
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 3.0 garlic cloves, peeled and minced

Instructions

  1. Season pork chops with salt and pepper
  2. Place in roasting pan
  3. Add rosemary, garlic in pan
  4. Pre-heat your oven to 425-degree F
  5. Bake for 10 minutes
  6. Lower heat to 350-degree F
  7. Roast for 25 minutes more
  8. Slice pork and divide on plates
  9. Drizzle pan juice all over
  10. Serve and enjoy!
