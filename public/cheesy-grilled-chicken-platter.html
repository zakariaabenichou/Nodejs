Cheesy Grilled Chicken Platter

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 large chicken breast, sliced half lenghtwise
  • 10.0 ounce spinach, frozen and drained
  • 3.0 ounce mozarella cheese, part skim
  • 0.5 a cup of roasted red peppers, cut in long strips
  • 1.0 teaspoon of olive oil
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, minced
  • Salt and pepper as needed

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree Fahrenheit
  2. Slice 3 chicken breast lengthwise
  3. Take a non-stick pan and grease with cooking spray
  4. Bake for 2-3 minutes each side
  5. Take another skillet and cook spinach and garlic in oil for 3 minutes
  6. Place chicken on an oven pan and top with spinach, roasted peppers and mozzarella
  7. Bake until the cheese melted
  8. Enjoy!
