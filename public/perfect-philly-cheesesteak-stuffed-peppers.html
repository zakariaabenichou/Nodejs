Perfect Philly Cheesesteak Stuffed Peppers

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 large bell peppers cut in half lengthwise
  • 8.0 oz. of thinly sliced roast beef or pastrami beef
  • 6.0 oz. of baby mushrooms, sliced
  • 8.0 slices of provolone or cheddar cheese
  • 2.0 tbsp of salted butter

Instructions

  1. Make sure that the bell peppers are cut lengthwise into halves and contain no seeds.
  2. In a pan, melt the butter and add the mushrooms. Saute for 3-4 minutes and remove from the heat.
  3. Take the bell pepper halves and start arranging one slice of cheese over the bottom layer of the pepper, then 2 slices of pastrami on each, and then a few mushrooms. Finish off each pepper boat with an extra slice of cheese on top.
  4. In a preheated oven (around 400F/200C), pop the peppers and cook for 18-20 minutes so that the peppers are cooked, and the cheese is melted.
  5. Let cool for 5 minutes and serve
