Lemon And Garlic Pork Platter

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Meduim | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 tablespoons butter
  • 4.0 pork steak, bone-in
  • 1.0 cup chicken stock
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Pinch of lemon pepper
  • 3.0 tablespoons coconut oil
  • 6.0 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
  • 2.0 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 8.0 ounces mushrooms, chopped
  • 1.0 lemon, sliced

Instructions

  1. Take a pan and place it over medium medium-high heat
  2. Add 2 tablespoons butter and 2 tablespoon oil, let it heat up
  3. Add pork steaks and season with salt and pepper
  4. Cook until browned on both sides; Transfer to plate
  5. Return pan to medium heat and add remaining butter, oil and half of stock
  6. Stir well and cook for 1 minute
  7. Add mushrooms, garlic, stir cook for 4 minutes
  8. Add lemon slices , remaining stock, slat, pepper and lemon pepper; Stir cook for 5 minutes
  9. Return steaks to pan and cook for 10 minutes
  10. Divide steak and sauce between serving platters; Enjoy!
