Double Berry Ice Pops

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 2 hrs chill time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 can use coconut cream
  • 0.75 c of unsweetened full-fat coconut milk
  • 2.0 drops liquid stevia
  • 0.5 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 0.25 c of mixed blueberries and blackberries

Instructions

  1. Take a food processor and add coconut cream, vanilla, and sweetener
  2. Process well and add mixed berries, pulse for a few times
  3. Pour into pop molds and freeze for 2 hours
  4. Serve and enjoy!

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 165.0 kcal
  • Fat: 17.0g
  • Carbs: 2.0g
  • Protein: 1.0g
