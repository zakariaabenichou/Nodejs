Spicy Wasabi Mayonnaise

Category: Keto Kitchen Staples And Dips Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup mayonnaise
  • 0.5 tablespoon wasabi paste

Instructions

  2. Mix well
  3. Let it chill and use as needed
