Brown Butter Duck Breas

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 25.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 whole 6 ounces duck breast, skin on
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 head radicchio, 4 ounces, core removed
  • 0.25 cup unsalted butter
  • 6.0 fresh sage leaves, sliced

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F; Pat duck breast dry with paper towel
  2. Season with salt and pepper; Place duck breast in skillet and place it over medium heat, sear for 3-4 minutes each side
  3. Turn breast over and transfer skillet to oven; Roast for 10 minutes (uncovered)
  4. Cut radicchio in half
  5. Remove and discard woody white core and thinly slice the leaves
  6. Keep them on the side; Remove skillet from oven
  7. Transfer duck breast, fat side up to cutting board and let it rest
  8. Re-heat your skillet over medium heat
  9. Add Unsalted butter, sage and cook for 3-4 minutes; Cut duck into 6 equal slices
  10. Divide radicchio between 2 plates, top with slices of duck breast and drizzle browned butter and sage; Enjoy!
