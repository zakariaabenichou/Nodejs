Skillet Based Kale And Avocado

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2.0 cups mushrooms, sliced
  • 5.0 ounces fresh kale, stemmed and sliced into ribbons
  • 1.0 avocado, sliced
  • 4.0 large eggs
  • Salt and pepper as needed

Instructions

  1. Take a large skillet and place it over medium heat
  2. Add a tablespoon olive oil
  3. Add mushrooms to pan and Saute for 3 minutes
  4. Take a medium bowl and massage kale with remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil (for about 1-2 minutes)
  5. Add kale to skillet and place them on top of mushrooms
  6. Place slices of avocado on top of kale
  7. Create 4 wells for eggs and crack each egg onto each hold
  8. Season eggs with salt and pepper
  9. Cover skillet and cook for 5 minutes
  10. Serve hot!
