Simple Green Goddess Dressing

Category: Keto Kitchen Staples And Dips Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons buttermilk
  • 0.25 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1.0 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1.0 garlic clove, minced
  • 1.0 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1.0 tablespoon fresh parsley leaves

Instructions

  1. Take a food processor and add butter, milk, yogurt, vinegar, apple cider, garlic, olive oil, parsley and blend well until combined

  3. Use as needed!
