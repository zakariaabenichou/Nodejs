Grilled Beef Short Loin

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds beef short loin
  • 2.0 thyme sprigs, chopped
  • 1.0 rosemary sprig, chopped
  • 1.0 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Sea salt and ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Place all of the above ingredients in a re-sealable zipper bag. Shake until the short beef loin is well coated on all sides.
  2. Cook on a preheated grill for 15 to 20 minutes, flipping once or twice during the cooking time.
  3. Let it stand for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
