Grilled Lime Shrimp

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 25.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 8.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound medium shirmp, peeled and deveined
  • 1.0 lime, juiced
  • 0.5 cup olive oil
  • 3.0 tablespoons Cajun seasoning

Instructions

  1. Take a re-sealable zip bag and add lime juice, Cajun seasoning, olive oil
  2. Add shrimp and shake it well, let it marinate for 20 minutes
  3. Pre-heat your outdoor grill to medium heat
  4. Lightly grease the grate
  5. Remove shrimp from marinade and cook for 2 minutes per side
  6. Serve and enjoy!
