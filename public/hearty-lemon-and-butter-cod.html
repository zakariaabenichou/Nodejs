Hearty Lemon And Butter Cod

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 tablespoons salted butter, divided
  • 4.0 thyme sprigs, fresh and divided
  • 4.0 teaspoons lemon juice, fresh and divided
  • 4.0 cod fillets, 6 ounces each
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Season cod fillets with salt on both side
  3. Take four pieces of foil, each foil should be 3 times bigger than fillets
  4. Divide fillets between the foils and top with butter, lemon juice, thyme
  5. Fold to form a pouch and transfer pouches to baking sheet
  6. Bake for 20 minutes
  7. Open and let the steam get out
  8. Serve and enjoy!
