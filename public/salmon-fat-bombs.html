Salmon Fat Bombs

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tbsp cream cheese, softened
  • 1.0 ounce smoked salmon
  • 2.0 tsp bagel seasoning

Instructions

  1. Take a medium bowl, place cream cheese and salmon in it, and stir until well combined.
  2. Shape the mixture into balls, roll them into bagel seasoning and then serve

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 48.0 kcal
  • Fat: 4.0g
  • Carbs: g
  • Protein: 0.5g
