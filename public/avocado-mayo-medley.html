Avocado Mayo Medley

Category: Keto Kitchen Staples And Dips Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 medium avocado, cut into chunks
  • 0.5 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
  • 2.0 tablespoons fresh cilantro
  • Pinch of salt
  • 0.25 cup olive oil

Instructions

  1. Take a food processor and add avocado, cayenne pepper, lime juice, salt and cilantro
  2. Mix until smooth
  3. Slowly incorporate olive oil, add 1 tablespoon at a time and keep processing in between additions
  4. Store and use as needed!
