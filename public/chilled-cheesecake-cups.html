Chilled Cheesecake Cups

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – chill time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2.0 oz heavy cream
  • 1.0 tsp Stevia Glycerite
  • 1.0 tsp Splenda
  • 1.0 tsp vanilla flavoring (Frontier Organic)

Instructions

  1. Combine all the ingredients.
  2. Whip until a pudding consistency is achieved.
  3. Divide into cups.
  4. Refrigerate until served!

  • Calories: 250.0 kcal
  • Fat: 20.0g
