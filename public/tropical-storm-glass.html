Tropical Storm Glass

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 tablespoon hemp seeds
  • 0.75 cup plain coconut yogurt
  • 1.0 fresh banana
  • 1.0 cup unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1.5 cups frozen papaya blend (mix of papaya, mango, strawberry, and pineapple)

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
