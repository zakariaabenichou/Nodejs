The Great Cabbage Slaw

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 ounces green and red cabbage, shredded and mixed
  • 4.0 ounces kale, chopped
  • 1.0 cup Keto-Friendly mayonnaise
  • 0.5 teaspoon each salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Add listed ingredients to a bowl and mix well using spatula
  2. Serve immediately or chilled
  3. Enjoy!
