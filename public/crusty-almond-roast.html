Crusty Almond Roast

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup keto almond flour
  • 4.0 tsp melted butter
  • 2.0 large eggs
  • 0.5 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Mix together the almond flour and butter
  2. Add in the eggs and salt and combine well to form a dough ball.
  3. Place the dough between two pieces of parchment paper. Roll out to 10" by 16" and 1/4 inch thick.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes at 350°F or until golden brown.
  5. Serve
