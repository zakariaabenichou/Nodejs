Sweet Almond Butter And Coconut Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 20 min chill time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 c of melted coconut oil
  • 1.5 tbsp of almond butter + 9 tbsp salted butter
  • 90.0 drops of liquid stevia
  • 3.0 tbsp cocoa

Instructions

  1. Take a bowl and add all of the listed ingredients
  2. Mix them well
  3. Pour scant 2 tablespoons of the mixture into as many muffin molds as you like
  4. Chill for 20 minutes and pop them out
  5. Serve and enjoy!
