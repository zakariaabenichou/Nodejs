Perfect Tuna Salad And Pickle Boats

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – 30 chill time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 18.0 oz canned and drained tuna
  • 6.0 large dill pickles
  • 0.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 0.25 cup sugar-free mayonnaise
  • 1.0 tsp onion powder

Instructions

  1. Mix the mayo, tuna, onion, and garlic powders in a bowl. Cut the pickles in half, lengthwise. Top each half with a tuna mixture.
  2. Place in the fridge for 30 minutes and serve

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 118.0 kcal
  • Fat: 10.0g
  • Carbs: 1.5g
  • Protein: 11.0g
