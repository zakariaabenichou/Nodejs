Cheesy Broccoli and Cauliflower

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound broccoli, chopped
  • 2.0 ounces butter
  • 8.0 ounces cauliflower, chopped
  • 5.33 ounces shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper , to taste
  • 4.0 teaspoons sour cream

Instructions

  1. Take a large skillet and melt butter
  2. Stir in all the vegetables
  3. Sauté until it turns into golden brown over medium-high heat
  4. Add all the remaining ingredients to the vegetable
  5. Mix well
  6. Serve warm and enjoy!
