Cilantro And Citrus Glass

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup ice
  • 2.0 cups arugula
  • 0.5 cup celery, diced
  • 1.0 grapefruit, peeled and segmented
  • 1.0 handful fresh cilantro leaves, chopped
  • 0.5 lemon, juiced
  • 0.5 cup of water

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth

  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
