Crust Free Mini Cheesecake

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 3 hours chill time

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 oz cream cheese
  • 0.25 c of sour cream
  • 2.0 large whole eggs
  • 0.3 c of natural sweetener
  • 0.25 tsp of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degrees F
  2. Take a medium bowl and add cream cheese, sour cream, eggs, sweetener, vanilla and blend until thoroughly mixed
  3. Place silicon liners in cups of a muffin tin
  4. Pour the batter in your liners and bake for 30 minutes
  5. Refrigerate for 3 hours
  6. Serve and enjoy!
