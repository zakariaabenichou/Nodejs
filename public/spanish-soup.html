Spanish Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – chill time

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1.0 cucumber, peeled, seeded, and chopped
  • 0.25 white onion, chopped
  • 0.5 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • tablespoons olive oil, garlic-flavored

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients and mix them in a blender.
  2. Blend until you get a smooth mixture.
  3. Close the lid and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Let it refrigerate for 1 hour.
  5. Garnish with bell pepper and chopped tomato.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
