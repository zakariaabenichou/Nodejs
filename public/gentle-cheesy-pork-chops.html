Gentle Cheesy Pork Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 ounces parmesan cheese
  • 0.5 cup almond flour
  • 7.0 center cut, pork chops
  • 1.0 whole egg
  • Bacon grease for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400-degree F
  2. Take a bowl and mix in cheese, flour and seasoning
  3. Take another bowl and mix in egg
  4. Dip pork chops in eggs, followed by a dip in the flour /cheese mix
  5. Fry in oil for 1-2 minutes each side (on medium heat)
  6. Transfer to a baking dish and bake in oven until golden brown
  7. Enjoy!
