Generous Fiery Tomato Salad

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 25.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup scallions, chopped
  • 1.0 pound cherry tomatoes
  • 3.0 teaspoons olive oil
  • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1.0 tablespoon red wine vinegar

Instructions

  1. Season tomatoes with spices and oil
  2. Heat your oven to 450-degree Fahrenheit
  3. Take a baking sheet and spread the tomatoes
  4. Bake for 15 minutes
  5. Stir and turn the tomatoes
  6. Then again, bake for 10 minutes
  7. Take a bowl and mix the roasted tomatoes with all the remaining ingredients
  8. Serve and enjoy!
