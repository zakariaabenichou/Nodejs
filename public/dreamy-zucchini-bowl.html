Dreamy Zucchini Bowl

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 onion, chopped
  • 3.0 zucchinis, cut into medium chunks
  • 2.0 tablespoons coconut milk
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4.0 cups chicken stock
  • 2.0 tablespoons coconut oil
  • Pinch of salt
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Take a pot and place it over medium heat
  2. Add oil and let it heat up
  3. Add zucchini, garlic, onion and stir
  4. Cook for 5 minutes
  5. Add stock, salt, pepper and stir
  6. Bring to a boil and lower down heat
  7. Simmer for 20 minutes Remove heat and add coconut milk
  8. Use an immersion blender until smooth
  9. Ladle into soup bowls and serve
  10. Enjoy!
