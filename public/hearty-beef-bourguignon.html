Hearty Beef Bourguignon

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 70.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds shoulder steak, cut into cubes
  • 1.0 tablespoon Herbs de Provence
  • 1.0 onion, chopped
  • 1.0 celery stalk, chopped
  • 1.0 cup red Burgundy wine

Instructions

  1. Heat up a lightly greased soup pot over a medium-high flame. Now brown the beef in batches until no longer pink.
  2. Add a splash of wine to deglaze your pan.
  3. Add the Herbs de Provence, onion, celery, and wine to the pot; pour in 3 cups of water and stir to combine well. Bring to a rapid boil; then, turn the heat to mediumlow.
  4. Cover and let it simmer for 1 hour 10 minutes. Serve over hot cauliflower rice if desired. Enjoy!
