Natural Nectarine

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup ice
  • Pinch of turmeric
  • 1.0 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1.0 tablespoon coconut flour
  • 0.25 cup brazil nuts
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach
  • 1.0 nectarine, pit removed
  • 1.0 cup seedless red grapes
  • 1.0 cup of coconut water

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
