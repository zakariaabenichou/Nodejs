Mesmerizing Garlic Bagels

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 6.0 whole eggs
  • 1.5 teaspoon Garlic powder
  • 0.3 cup butter, melted
  • 0.5 teaspoon salt
  • 0.5 cup coconut flour, sifted
  • 0.5 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Grease bagel pan and keep it on the side
  3. Whisk in eggs, garlic powder, butter, salt to a bowl and keep it on the side
  4. Add coconut flour and baking powder to egg mix, mix well until incorporated and a batter forms with no lumps
  5. Pour batter into bagel pan
  6. Bake for 15 minutes
  7. Remove from oven and let them cool
  8. Serve and enjoy!
