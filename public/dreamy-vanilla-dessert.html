Dreamy Vanilla Dessert

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + chill time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup extra virgin coconut oil, softened
  • 0.5 cup coconut butter, softened
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Seeds from 1/2 a vanilla bean

Instructions

  1. Whisk the ingredients in an easy-to-pour cup.
  2. Pour into a lined cupcake or loaf pan.
  3. Refrigerate for 20 minutes. Top with lemon zest.
  4. Serve!
