Cheese And Bacon Balls

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + chill time

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 ounces bacon
  • 6.0 ounces brie cheese
  • 1.0 chili pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 0.25 tsp parsley flakes
  • 0.5 tsp paprika

Instructions

  1. Set a pan over medium heat and fry the bacon until crispy; then crush it.
  2. Place the other ingredients in a bowl and mix to combine with the bacon grease.
  3. Refrigerate the mixture for 20 minutes.
  4. Remove and form balls from the mixture. Set the bacon on a plate and roll the balls around to coat
  5. Serve and enjoy!
