Balsamic Grilled Zucchini

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 zucchini, sliced lengthwise1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 1.0 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2.0 tbsp balsamic vinegar2 tsp avocado oil
  • Seasoning:
  • 0.25 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Take a griddle pan, place it over medium-low heat, brush it with oil and let it preheat
  2. Meanwhile, brush zucchini slices with oil and then sprinkle with garlic powder, Italian seasoning, and salt.
  3. Place zucchini slices on the griddle pan and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, then brush zucchini with vinegar and cook for another minute.
  4. Serve and enjoy!
