The Perfect Mediterranean Turkey Cutlets

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1.5 poundturkey cutlets
  • 0.25 cup low carb flour mix
  • 0.5 teaspoon Greek seasoning
  • 0.5 teaspoon turmeric powder

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, mix the turkey cutlets with turmeric powder, low carb flour mix, and Greek seasoning
  2. Put a frying pan on fire, then add the oil to heat.
  3. Add the cutlets and cook for 5 minutes on each side under medium-low heat.
  4. Serve!
