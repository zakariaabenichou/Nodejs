Healthy Tuna Croquettes

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 4.0 mins –

Cook Time: 9.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 can tuna, drained
  • 1.0 whole large egg
  • 8.0 tablespoons parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2.0 tablespoons flax meal
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 tablespoons onion, minced

Instructions

  1. Add all of the ingredients to a blender (except flax meal) and pulse them mixture into a crunchy texture
  2. Form patties using the mixture
  3. Dip both sides of the patties in flax meals and fry them in hot oil until both sides are browned well

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 105.0 kcal
  • Fat: 5.0g
  • Carbs: 2.0g
  • Protein: 14.0g
