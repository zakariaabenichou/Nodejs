Crispy Cold Coconut Bombs

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 1-2 hrs chill time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 14.5 ounces coconut milk
  • 0.75 c of coconut oil
  • 1.0 c of unsweetened coconut flakes
  • 20.0 drops of stevia

Instructions

  1. Microwave your coconut oil for 20 seconds in the microwave
  2. Mix in coconut milk and stevia in the hot oil
  3. Stir in coconut flakes and pour the mixture into molds
  4. Let it chill for 60 minutes in the fridge
  5. Serve and enjoy!
