Easy Portobello Mushrooms

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 cherry tomatoes
  • 2.0 ounces scallions
  • 4.0 portobello mushrooms
  • 4.25 ounces butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Take a large skillet and melt butter over medium heat
  2. Add mushrooms and Sauté for 3 minutes
  3. Stir in cherry tomatoes and scallions
  4. Sauté for 5 minutes
  5. Season accordingly
  6. Sauté until veggies are tender
  7. Enjoy!
