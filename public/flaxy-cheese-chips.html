Flaxy Cheese Chips

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cup cheddar cheese
  • 4.0 tbsp ground flaxseed meal
  • Seasonings of your choice

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F/220°C.
  2. Spoon 2 tablespoons of cheddar cheese into a mound onto a non-stick pad.
  3. Spread out a pinch of flax seed on each chip.
  4. Season and bake for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
