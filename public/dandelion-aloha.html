Dandelion Aloha

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup ice
  • 1.0 cup pineapple, chopped
  • 2.0 cups dandelion greens
  • 1.0 cup unsweetened almond milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
