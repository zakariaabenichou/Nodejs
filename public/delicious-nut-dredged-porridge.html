Delicious Nut Dredged Porridge

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup cashew nuts, raw and unsalted
  • 1.0 cup pecan, halved
  • 2.0 tablespoons stevia
  • 4.0 teaspoons coconut oil, melted
  • 2.0 cups water

Instructions

  1. Chop the nuts in food processor and form a smooth paste
  2. Add water, oil, stevia to nuts paste and transfer the mix to a saucepan
  3. Stir cook for 5 minutes on high heat
  4. Lower heat to low and simmer for 10 minutes
  5. Serve warm and enjoy!

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 260.0 kcal
  • Fat: 22.0g
  • Carbs: 12.0g
  • Protein: 6.0g
