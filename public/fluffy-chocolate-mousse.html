Fluffy Chocolate Mousse

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 can (14.5 ounces) coconut cream, chilled
  • 3.0 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 0.25 cup Swerve
  • 1.0 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Take a large sized mixing bowl and add coconut cream, whip with hand mixer
  2. Keep whipping for 3 minutes until fluffy
  3. Fold in cocoa powder, vanilla, swerve and mix
  4. Serve immediately
