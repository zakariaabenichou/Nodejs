Amazing Buffalo Lettuce Wraps

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 35.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 chicken breast, boneless and cubed
  • 20.0 slices of butter lettuce leaves
  • 0.75 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1.0 avocado, chopped
  • 0.25 cup green onions, diced
  • 0.5 a cup of ranch dressing
  • 0.75 cup hot sauce

Instructions

  1. Take a mixing bowl and add chicken cubes and hot sauce, mix
  2. Place in fridge and let it marinate for 30 minutes
  3. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree Fahrenheit
  4. Place coated chicken on cookie pan and bake for 9 minutes
  5. Assemble lettuce serving cups with equal amounts of lettuce, green onions, tomatoes, ranch dressing and cubed chicken
  6. Serve and enjoy!
