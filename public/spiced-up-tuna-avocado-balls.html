Spiced Up Tuna Avocado Balls

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 avocados halved
  • 0.5 lbof sushi-grade ahi tuna (or smoked tuna if you can't find any)
  • 2.0 tbsp of mayo
  • sriracha sauce
  • 1.0 tsp of toasted sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Combine in a small bowl the tuna with the mayo, sriracha sauce, and toasted sesame seeds.
  2. Scoop and distribute the mixture onto the avocado halves.
  3. Add some extra sriracha sauce optionally on top

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 256.0 kcal
  • Fat: 17.0g
  • Carbs: 8.0g
  • Protein: 15.0g
