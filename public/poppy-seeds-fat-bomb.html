Poppy Seeds Fat Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 ounces cream cheese, soft
  • 3.0 tablespoons erythritol
  • 1.0 tablespoon poppy seeds
  • 1.0 lemon zest
  • 2.0 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 4.0 tablespoons sour cream

Instructions

  1. Add listed ingredients to a bowl and mix using a hand mixer on low.
  2. Once mixed, mix for 3 minutes on a medium-high setting.
  3. Spoon mixture into mini cupcake cases and chill for 1 hour.
  4. Enjoy once done!
