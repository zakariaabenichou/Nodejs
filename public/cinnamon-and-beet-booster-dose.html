Cinnamon And Beet Booster Dose

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1.0 medium cucumber, diced
  • 1.0 medium beet, scrubbed and halved
  • 1.0 cup kale, chopped
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach, chopped

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy
