Dreamy Hot Fudge

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 5.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup salted butter
  • 4.0 ounces dark chocolate
  • 2.0 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1.0 cup swerve
  • 1.0 cup heavy whip cream
  • 2.0 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Take a medium saucepan and place it over medium heat
  2. Add butter and chocolate and melt
  3. Add cocoa powder and sweetener
  4. Whisk for 3-5 minutes until everything dissolves
  5. Add cream and bring to a boil
  6. Stir
  7. Lower down heat to low and add vanilla and salt
  8. Remove heat
  9. Let it sit for 5 minutes
  10. Serve hot and enjoy!
