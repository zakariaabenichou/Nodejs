Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

  • 1.0 pound kohlrabi, peeled and shredded
  • Fresh parsley
  • 1.0 cup mayonnaise (Keto-Friendly)
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions
