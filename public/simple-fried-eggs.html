Simple Fried Eggs

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 eggs
  • 2.0 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.12 tsp ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Take a skillet pan, place it over medium heat, add butter and when it has melted, crack eggs in the pan.
  2. Cook eggs for 3 to 5 minutes until fried to the desired level, then transfer the eggs to serving plates and sprinkle with salt and black pepper.
  3. Serve.
