Simple Garlic And Lemon Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 avocado, pitted and chopped
  • 1.0 cucumber, chopped
  • 2.0 bunches spinach
  • 1.5 cups watermelon, chopped
  • 1.0 bunch cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Juice from 2 lemons
  • 0.5 cup coconut aminos
  • 0.5 cup lime juice

Instructions

  1. Add cucumber, avocado to your blender and pulse well
  2. Add cilantro, spinach and watermelon and blend
  3. Add lemon, lime juice and coconut amino
  4. Pulse few more times
  5. Transfer to soup bowl and enjoy!
