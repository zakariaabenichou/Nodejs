Tasty Roasted Broccoli

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 cups broccoli florets
  • 1.0 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Add broccoli in a zip bag alongside oil and shake until coated
  3. Add seasoning and shake again
  4. Spread broccoli out on baking sheet, bake for 20 minutes
  5. Let it cool and serve
  6. Enjoy!
