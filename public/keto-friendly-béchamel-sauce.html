Keto-Friendly Béchamel Sauce

Category: Keto Kitchen Staples And Dips Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 1.75 cups heavy whip cream
  • 7.0 ounces cream cheese
  • 0.5 teaspoon salt
  • 0.25 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 0.25 teaspoon nutmeg

Instructions

  1. Add listed ingredients to a non-stick saucepan and bring to a boil, making sure to keep stirring it continuously
  2. Lower heat to low and let it simmer for a few minutes until it reaches your desired consistency
  3. Once done, season with salt and pepper and use as needed!
