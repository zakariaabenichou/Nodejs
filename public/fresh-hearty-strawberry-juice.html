Fresh Hearty Strawberry Juice

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 cups fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 2.0 cups chilled water
  • 1.0 teaspoon fresh lime juice-2 drops liquid stevia
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients to a blender and pulse until finely pureed.
  2. Through a cheesecloth-lined strainer, strain the juice and transfer it into serving glasses.
  3. Serve immediately.
