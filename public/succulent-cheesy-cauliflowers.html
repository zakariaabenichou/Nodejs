Succulent Cheesy Cauliflowers

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 25.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cauliflower head
  • 0.25 cup butter, cut into small pieces
  • 1.0 teaspoon Keto-Friendly Mayo
  • 1.0 tablespoon Keto-Friendly Mustard
  • 0.5 cup parmesan cheese, grated

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 390 degrees F
  2. Add mayo and mustard in a bowl
  3. Add cauliflower to mayo mix and toss
  4. Spread cauliflower in a baking dish and top with butter
  5. Sprinkle cheese on top
  6. Bake for 25 minutes
  7. Serve and enjoy!
