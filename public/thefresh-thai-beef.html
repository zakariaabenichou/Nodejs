TheFresh Thai Beef

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup beef stock
  • 4.0 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 0.25 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 0.25 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1.0 tablespoon coconut aminos
  • 1.5 teaspoons lemon pepper
  • 1.0 pound beef steak, cut into strips
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 3.0 green onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. Take a bowl and add peanut butter, stock, aminos, lemon pepper and sti
  2. Keep the mixture on the side
  3. Take a pan and place it over medium-high heat
  4. Add beef, season with salt, pepper, onion, garlic powder
  5. Cook for 7 minutes
  6. Add green pepper, stir cook for 3 minutes
  7. Add peanut sauce and green onions
  8. Stir cook for 1 minute
  9. Divide between platters and serve
  10. Enjoy!
