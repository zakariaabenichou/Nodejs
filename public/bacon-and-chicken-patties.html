Bacon And Chicken Patties

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 oz can chicken breast
  • 4.0 slices bacon
  • 0.25 cup parmesan cheese
  • 1.0 large egg
  • 3.0 tbsp keto coconut flour

Instructions

  1. Cook the bacon until crispy.
  2. Chop the chicken and bacon together in a food processor until fine.
  3. Add in the parmesan, egg, coconut flour, and mix.
  4. Make the patties by hand and fry on medium heat in a pan with some oil.
  5. Once browned, flip over, continue cooking, and lie them to drain.
  6. Serve!
