Refreshing Watermelon Breather

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 key lime, juiced and zest
  • 0.5 watermelon, peeled, deseeded, and cubed

Instructions

  1. Add the listed ingredients to your blender/food processor and blend until smooth
  2. Serve chilled and enjoy!
