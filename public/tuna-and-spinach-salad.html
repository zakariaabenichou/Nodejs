Tuna And Spinach Salad

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 oz of spinach leaves
  • 2.0 oz tuna, packed in water
  • 0.25 tsp ground black pepper
  • 0.25 tsp sea salt
  • 2.0 tbsp coconut oil, melted

Instructions

  1. Take a salad bowl, place spinach leaves in it, drizzle with 1 tbsp oil, sprinkle with 1/8 tsp of salt and black pepper, and then toss until mixed.
  2. Top with tuna, sprinkle with remaining salt and black pepper, drizzle with oil and then serve

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 155.0 kcal
  • Fat: 62.0g
  • Carbs: 1.3g
  • Protein: 0.7g
