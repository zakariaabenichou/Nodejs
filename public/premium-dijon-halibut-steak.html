Premium Dijon Halibut Steak

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 6-oz fresh or thawed halibut steak
  • 1.0 tbsp butter
  • 1.0 tbsp lemon juice
  • 0.5 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1.0 tsp fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Heat the butter, basil, lemon juice, and mustard in a small saucepan to make a glaze.
  2. Brush both sides of the halibut steak with the mixture.
  3. Grill the fish for 10 minutes over medium heat until tender and flakey.
