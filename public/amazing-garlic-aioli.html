Amazing Garlic Aioli

Category: Keto Kitchen Staples And Dips Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup mayonnaise
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1.0 tablespoon fresh-flat leaf Italian parsley, chopped
  • 1.0 teaspoon chives, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Add mayo, garlic, parsley, lemon juice, chives and season with salt and pepper
  2. Blend until combined well
  3. Pour into refrigerator and chill for 30 minutes
  4. Serve and enjoy using Keto Friendly bread!
