1 Minute Keto Muffin

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 1.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 whole egg
  • 2.0 teaspoon coconut flour
  • A pinch of baking soda
  • A pinch salt
  • Coconut oil, for grease

Instructions

  1. Grease ramekin dish with coconut oil.
  2. Keep it aside.
  3. Take a bowl and add ingredients and mix well.
  4. Pour batter into a ramekin.
  5. Place into microwave for 1 minute on HIGH.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
