Excellent Zucchini Sage Cakes

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 12.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound zucchinis, grated, and excess water drained
  • Salt and black pepper to the taste
  • 1.0 tablespoon almond flour
  • 1.0 egg, whisked
  • 1.0 tablespoon sage, chopped2 tablespoons olive oil

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine the zucchinis with the flour and the other ingredients except for the oil, stir well and shape medium cakes out of this mix.
  2. Heat up a pan with the oil over medium heat, add the cakes, cook them for 5-6 minutes on each side, drain excess grease on paper towels
  3. Divide the cakes between plates and serve for breakfast
