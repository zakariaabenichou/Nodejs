Sweet And Savory Grilled Chicken

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 teaspoon dry mustard
  • 1.0 teaspoon light brown sugar
  • 1.5 teaspoon onion powder
  • 0.75 pound skinless chicken breast
  • Kosher salt & White pepper

Instructions

  1. Set the grill to preheat at medium-high temperatures as you add some greasing
  2. In a small bowl, add onion powder, dry mustard, salt, brown sugar, and white pepper and mix well
  3. Pass the chicken meat through the mixture to coat evenly.
  4. Grill the chicken for 6 minutes on each side
  5. Serve!
