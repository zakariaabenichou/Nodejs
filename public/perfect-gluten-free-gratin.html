Perfect Gluten-Free Gratin

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 25.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 cups raw cauliflower florets
  • 4.0 tbsp butter
  • 0.3 cup heavy whipping cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 5.0 deli slices pepper jack cheese

Instructions

  1. Combine the cauliflower, butter, cream, salt, and pepper and microwave on medium for 20 minutes, or until tender.
  2. Mash with a fork—season to your liking.
  3. Lay the slices of cheese across the top of the cauliflower.
  4. Cook inside your microwave for an additional 3 minutes, depending on the power of your microwave
  5. Serve!
