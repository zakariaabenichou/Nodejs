The Brewed Coffee Surprise

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 heaped tbsp flaxseed, ground
  • 100.0 ml cooking cream 35% fat
  • 0.5 tsp cocoa powder, dark and unsweetened
  • 1.0 tbsp goji berries
  • Freshly brewed coffee

Instructions

  1. Mix together the flaxseeds, cream and cocoa, and coffee.
  2. Season with goji berries.
  3. Serve!
