Tamari Steak Salad

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 large bunches salad greens
  • 9.0 ounces beef steak
  • 0.5 red bell pepper, siced
  • 8.0 cherry tomatoes, cut into halves
  • 4.0 radishes, sliced
  • 4.0 tablespoons olive oil
  • 0.5 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 2.0 ounces gluten free tamari sauce
  • Salt as needed

Instructions

  1. Marinate steak in tamari sauce
  2. Make salad by adding bell pepper, tomatoes, radishes, salad green, oil, salt and lemon juice to a bowl, and toss them well
  3. Grill the steak to your desired doneness and transfer steak on top of the salad platter
  4. Let it sit for 1 minute and cut it crosswise
  5. Serve and enjoy!
