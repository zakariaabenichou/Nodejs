Chilled Minty Avocado Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – chill time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 avocado, ripe
  • 1.0 cup coconut milk, chilled
  • 2.0 romaine lettuce leaves
  • 20.0 mint leaves, fresh
  • 1.0 tablespoon lime juice

Instructions

  1. Turn on your slow cooker and add all the ingredients into it.
  2. Mix them in a food processor.
  3. Make a smooth mixture.
  4. Let it chill for 10 minutes.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
