Lovely Lemonade Fat Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 whole lemon
  • 4.0 oz of cream cheese
  • 2.0 oz of butter
  • 2.0 tsp of natural sweetener

Instructions

  1. Take a fine grater and zest your lemon
  2. Squeeze lemon juice into a bowl alongside the zest
  3. Add butter, cream cheese to a bowl and add zest, salt, sweetener, and juice
  4. Stir well using a hand mixer until smooth
  5. Spoon mix into molds and let it freeze for 2 hours
  6. Serve and enjoy!
