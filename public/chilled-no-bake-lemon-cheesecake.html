Chilled No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 60 minutes Chill Time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 ounces cream cheese
  • 2.0 ounces full-fat cream
  • 1.0 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Few drops of vanilla extract
  • Peel ½ lemon, grated

Instructions

  1. Add cream, cream cheese, and mix well.
  2. Add the remaining ingredients.
  3. Transfer the mixture to your fridge.
  4. Keep it for 1 hour in the fridge.
  5. Serve and enjoy!

  • Calories: 220.0 kcal
  • Fat: 17.0g
  • Carbs: 2.0g
  • Protein: 6.0g
