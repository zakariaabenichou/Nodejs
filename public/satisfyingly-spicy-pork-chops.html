Satisfyingly Spicy Pork Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 250.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup lime juice
  • 4.0 pork rib chops
  • 1.0 tablespoon coconut oil, melted
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
  • 1.0 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1.0 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2.0 teaspoons cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 0.5 teaspoon hot pepper sauce
  • Mango, sliced

Instructions

  1. Take a bowl and mix in lime juice, oil, garlic, cumin, cinnamon, chili powder, salt, pepper, hot pepper sauce
  2. Whisk well
  3. Add pork chops and toss
  4. Keep it on the side and let it refrigerate for 4 hours
  5. Pre-heat your grill to medium and transfer pork chops to pre-heated grill
  6. Grill for 7 minutes, flip and cook for 7 minutes more
  7. Divide between serving platters and serve with mango slices
  8. Enjoy!
