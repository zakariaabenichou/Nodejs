The Heartthrob Lemon Mousse

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – chill time

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup coconut cream
  • 8.0 ounces cream cheese, soft
  • 0.25 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 3.0 pinches salt
  • 1.0 teaspoon lemon liquid stevia

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degree F
  2. Grease a ramekin with butter
  3. Beat cream, cream cheese, fresh lemon juice, salt and lemon liquid stevia in a mixer
  4. Pour batter into ramekin
  5. Bake for 10 minutes then transfer mouse to serving glass
  6. Let it chill for 2 hours and serve
  7. Enjoy!
