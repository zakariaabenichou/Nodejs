Powerful Green Frenzy

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup ice
  • 2.0 tablespoons almond butter
  • 1.0 teaspoon spirulina
  • 3.0 teaspoon fresh ginger
  • 1.5 frozen bananas, sliced
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach, chopped
  • 1.0 cup kale
  • 1.5 cups unsweetened almond milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth

  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
