Icy Berry Popsicles

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 2 Hours Chill Time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup mixed blackberries and blueberries
  • 2.0 cups coconut cream
  • 2.0 teaspoons stevia

Instructions

  1. Blend the listed ingredients into your blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour the mix into popsicle molds.
  4. Keep in the fridge for 2 hours.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
