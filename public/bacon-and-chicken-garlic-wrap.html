Bacon And Chicken Garlic Wrap

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 chicken fillet, cut into small cubes
  • 9.0 thin slices bacon, cut to fit cubes
  • 6.0 garlic cloves, minced

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Line a baking tray with aluminum foil
  3. Add minced garlic to a bowl and rub each chicken piece with it
  4. Wrap bacon piece around each garlic chicken bite
  5. Secure with toothpick
  6. Transfer bites to baking sheet, keeping a little bit of space between them
  7. Bake for about 15-20 minutes until crispy
  8. Serve and enjoy!
