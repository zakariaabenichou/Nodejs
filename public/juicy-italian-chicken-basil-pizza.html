Juicy Italian Chicken Basil Pizza

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups grated mozzarella cheese
  • 1.0 lb ground chicken
  • 1.0 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1.0 cup tomato sauce
  • 0.5 cup fresh basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 390 F and line a round pizza pan with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, mix ground chicken, Italian seasoning, and 1 cup of mozzarella cheese.
  3. Spread the pizza "dough" on the pizza pan and bake for 18 minutes—spread tomato sauce on top.
  4. Scatter the mozzarella cheese and basil all over and bake for 15 minutes.
  5. Slice and serve.
