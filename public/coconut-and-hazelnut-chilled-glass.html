Coconut And Hazelnut Chilled Glass

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup coconut milk
  • 0.25 cup hazelnuts, chopped
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1.0 pack stevia

Instructions

  1. Add listed ingredients to blender
  2. Blend until you have a smooth and creamy texture
  3. Serve chilled and enjoy!
