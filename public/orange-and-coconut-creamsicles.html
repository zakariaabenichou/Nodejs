Orange And Coconut Creamsicles

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 3 Hours Chill Time

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup of coconut oil
  • 0.5 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 4.0 ounces cream cheese
  • 1.0 teaspoon orange mix

Instructions

  1. Add the listed ingredients to a bowl.
