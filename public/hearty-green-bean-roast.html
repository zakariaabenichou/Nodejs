Hearty Green Bean Roast

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 whole egg
  • 2.0 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 pound fresh green beans
  • 5.5 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Take a bowl and whisk in eggs with oil and spices
  3. Add beans and mix well
  4. Stir in parmesan cheese and pour the mix into baking pan (lined with parchment paper)
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes
  6. Serve warm and enjoy!
