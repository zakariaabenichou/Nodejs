Wild Mushroom Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 10.0 oz of wild mushrooms
  • 0.25 cup salted butter
  • 1.0 tbsp of garlic, minced
  • 5.0 cups of vegetable broth
  • 0.5 cup of fresh cream

Instructions

  1. Heat a pan with the butter and saute the mushrooms with the garlic for 3-4 minutes.
  2. Transfer to a large saucepan and add the vegetable broth. Bring to a boil and reduce the heat to simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Add the creme fresh towards the last two minutes of cooking
  4. Let the soup cool for 5 minutes and using an immersion blender blend until the soup is silky smooth with no visible parts of mushrooms. You should end up with a grayish_x0002_brown sort of color.
  5. Transfer into soup bowls and add extra doses of cream or parsley on top optionally, for garnish
