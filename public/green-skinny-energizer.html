Green Skinny Energizer

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 ipe mango, pitted and sliced
  • 1.0 cup kale, chopped
  • 3.0 cups baby spinach
  • 1.0 cup of coconut water

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
