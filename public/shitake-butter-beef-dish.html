Shitake Butter Beef Dish

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 cups shitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 4.0 ribeye steaks
  • 2.0 tbsp butter
  • 2.0 tsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Rub the steaks with salt and pepper and cook for 4 minutes per side. Set aside.
  2. Melt butter in the pan and cook the shitakes for 4 minutes.
  3. Pour the butter and mushrooms over the steak.
