Crunchy Garlic Bread Stick

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 8.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup butter, softened
  • 1.0 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2.0 cups almond flour
  • 0.5 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1.0 tablespoon Psyllium husk powder
  • 0.25 teaspoon salt
  • 3.0 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1.0 egg
  • 0.25 cup boiling water

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400-degree F
  2. Line baking sheet with parchment paper and keep it on the side
  3. Beat butter with garlic powder and keep it on the side
  4. Add almond flour, baking powder, husk, salt in a bowl and mix in butter and egg, mix well
  5. Pour boiling water in the mix and stir until you have a nice dough
  6. Divide the dough into 8 balls and roll into breadsticks
  7. Place on baking sheet and bake for 15 minutes
  8. Brush each stick with garlic butter and bake for 5 minutes more
  9. Serve and enjoy!
