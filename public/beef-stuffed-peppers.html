Beef Stuffed Peppers

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 50.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound ground beef
  • 1.0 garlic clove, minced
  • Sea salt and ground black pepper, to taste
  • 0.25 cup cream of onion soup
  • 0.5 teaspoon paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a saute pan over moderate heat. Once hot, sear the ground beef for 5 to 6 minutes, turning once or twice to ensure even cooking.
  2. Add in the cream of onion soup, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Cook for a further 3 minutes until heated through. The meat thermometer should register 145 degrees F.
  3. Serve in individual plates garnished with freshly snipped chives if desired. Enjoy!
