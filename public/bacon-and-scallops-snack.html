Bacon And Scallops Snack

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 6.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 scallops
  • 12.0 thin bacon slices
  • 12.0 toothpicks
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 0.5 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Heat a skillet on high heat while drizzling in the oil.
  2. Wrap each scallop with a piece of thinly cut bacon—secure with a toothpick.
  3. Season to taste.
  4. Cook for 3 minutes per side.
  5. Serve!
