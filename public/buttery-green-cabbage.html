Buttery Green Cabbage

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds shredded green cabbage
  • 3.0 ounces butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 dollop, whipped cream

Instructions

  1. Take a large skillet and place it over medium heat
  2. Add butter and melt
  3. Stir in cabbage and Saute for 15 minutes
  4. Season accordingly
  5. Serve with a dollop of cream
  6. Enjoy!
