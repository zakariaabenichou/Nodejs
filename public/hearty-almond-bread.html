Hearty Almond Bread

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 60.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 8.0

Ingredients

  • 3.0 cups almond flour
  • 1.0 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2.0 teaspoons baking powder
  • 0.25 teaspoon salt
  • 0.25 cup almond milk
  • 0.5 cup + 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3.0 whole eggs

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 300 degree F
  2. Take an 9x5 inch loaf pan and grease, keep it on the side
  3. Add listed ingredients to a bowl and pour the batter into the loaf pan
  4. Bake for 60 minutes
  5. Once baked, remove from oven and let it cool
  6. Slice and serve!
