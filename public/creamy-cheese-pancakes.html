Creamy Cheese Pancakes

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 12.0 mins –

Difficulty: | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 organic eggs
  • 2.0 ounces cream cheese, softened 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon 1 packet stevia
  • Olive oil nonstick cooking spray

Instructions

  1. Place all the ingredients in a blender and pulse until smooth.
  2. Transfer the mixture into a bowl and set aside for about 2-3 minutes.
  3. Grease a large nonstick skillet with cooking spray and heat over medium heat.
  4. Add 1/4 of the mixture and tilt the pan to spread it in an even layer.
  5. Cook for about 2 minutes or until golden brown.
  6. Flip the side and cook for about 1 more minute.
  7. Repeat with the remaining mixture.
  8. Serve warm
