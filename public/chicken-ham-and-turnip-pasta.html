Chicken Ham And Turnip Pasta

Category: Poultry Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 6.0 slices chicken ham, chopped
  • 1.0 lb turnips, spiralized
  • 1.0 tbsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4.0 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 F. Pour turnips into a bowl; add in the paprika, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
  2. Spread the mixture on a greased baking sheet, scatter ham on top, and drizzle with olive oil.
  3. Bake for 10 minutes until golden brown
