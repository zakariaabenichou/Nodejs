Spicy Bread Loaf

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 55.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings:

Ingredients

  • 6.0 big whole eggs
  • 3.0 big jalapenos
  • 4.0 ounces turkey bacon
  • 0.5 cup ghee
  • 0.25 teaspoon baking soda
  • 0.25 teaspoon salt
  • 0.5 cup coconut flour

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Cut 3 big jalapenos and cut the jalapenos in slices, cut turkey bacon into thick slices
  3. Place jalapenos and bacon on baking tray, roast for 30 minutes
  4. Flip ad bake for 5 minutes more
  5. Remove seeds from jalapeno and add jalapeno and bacon slices to food processor
  6. Take a big bowl and add eggs, ghee, ¼ cup water
  7. Mix well and add coconut flour, baking soda, salt and stir
  8. Add jalapeno and bacon mix
  9. Use a bit of ghee to grease the loaf pan. Pour batter into loaf pan
  10. Bake for 40 minutes. Enjoy!
