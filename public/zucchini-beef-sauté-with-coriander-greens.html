Zucchini Beef Sauté With Coriander Greens

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 10.0 ounces beef, sliced into 1-2 inch strips
  • 1.0 zuccchini, cut into 2 inch strips
  • 0.25 cup parsley, chopped
  • 3.0 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2.0 tablespoons tamari sauce
  • 4.0 tablespoons avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Add 2 tablespoons avocado oil in a frying pan over high heat
  2. Place strips of beef and brown for a few minutes on high heat
  3. Once the meat is brown, add zucchini strips and Saute until tender
  4. Once tender, add tamari sauce, garlic, parsley
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!
