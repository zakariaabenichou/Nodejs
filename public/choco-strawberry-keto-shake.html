Choco Strawberry Keto Shake

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup heavy cream, liquid
  • 1.0 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1.0 pack stevia
  • 0.5 cup strawberry, sliced
  • 1.0 tablespoons coconut flakes, unsweetened
  • 1.5 cups water

Instructions

  1. Add listed ingredients to blender
  2. Blend until you have a smooth and creamy texture
  3. Serve chilled with some Keto-Friendly whipped cream and berries on top
  4. Enjoy!
