Generous Stuffed Salmon Avocado

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 ripe organic avocado
  • 2.0 ounces wild caught smoked salmon
  • 1.0 ounce fresh goat cheese
  • 2.0 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt as needed

Instructions

  1. Cut avocado in half and deseed
  2. Add rest of the ingredients to a food processor and process until coarsely chopped
  3. Place mixture into avocado
  4. Serve and enjoy!
