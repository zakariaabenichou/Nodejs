Creative Lemon And Broccoli Dish

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 heads brococli, separated into florets
  • 2.0 teaspoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1.0 teaspoon salt
  • 0.5 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1.0 garlic clove, minced
  • 0.5 teaspoon lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 400 degree F
  2. Take a large sized bowl and add broccoli florets
  3. Drizzle olive oil and season with pepper, salt and garlic
  4. Spread broccoli out in single even layer on a baking sheet
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes until fork tender
  6. Squeeze lemon juice on top
  7. Serve and enjoy!
