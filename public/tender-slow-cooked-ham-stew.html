Tender Slow Cooked Ham Stew

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 240.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 ounces cheddar cheese, grated
  • 14.0 ounces chicken stock
  • 0.5 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4.0 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
  • 0.25 cup heavy cream
  • 3.0 cups ham, chopped
  • 16.0 ounces cauliflower florets
  • 1.0 cup carrot, sliced into thin rings

Instructions

  1. Add ham, stock, cheese, cauliflower, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, garlic, carrot, heavy cream to Slow Cooker
  2. Stir well
  3. Place lid and cook on HIGH for 4 hours
  4. Stir and divide between bowls
  5. Serve and enjoy!
