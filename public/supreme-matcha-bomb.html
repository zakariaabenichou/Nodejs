Supreme Matcha Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 100.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 10.0

Ingredients

  • 0.75 cup hemp seeds
  • 0.5 cup coconut oil
  • 2.0 tablespoons coconut butter
  • 1.0 teaspoon Matcha powder
  • 2.0 tablespoons vanilla bean extract
  • 0.5 teaspoon mint extract
  • Liquid stevia

Instructions

  1. Take your blender/food processor and add hemp seeds, coconut oil, Matcha, vanilla extract and stevia
  2. Blend until you have a nice batter and divide into silicon molds
  3. Melt coconut butter and drizzle on top
  4. Let the cups chill and enjoy!
