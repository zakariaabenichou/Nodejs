Cashew And Almond Butter

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 cup almonds, blanched
  • 0.33 cup cashew nuts
  • 2.0 tablespoons coconut oil
  • Salt as needed
  • 0.5 teaspoon cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degree F
  2. Bake almonds and cashews for 12 minutes
  3. Let them cool
  4. Transfer to food processor and add remaining ingredients
  5. Add oil and keep blending until smooth
  6. Serve and enjoy!
