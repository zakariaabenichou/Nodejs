Cool Bacon And Cheese Rolls

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 50.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 oz mozzarella cheese, sliced, full-fat
  • 4.0 slices of bacon

Instructions

  1. Take a skillet pan, place it over medium heat and when hot, add bacon slices and cook for 3 minutes per side until crisp.
  2. When done, transfer bacon to the cutting board, cool for 5 minutes, and then chop.
  3. Cut cheese into thin slices, top with chopped bacon, and then roll the cheese.
  4. Serve.

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 165.0 kcal
  • Fat: 12.0g
  • Carbs: 0.8g
  • Protein: 0.8g
