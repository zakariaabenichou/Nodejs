Denver Omelete

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 4.0 mins –

Cook Time: 1.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 1.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons butter
  • 0.25 cup onion, chopped
  • 0.25 cup green bell pepper, diced
  • 0.25 cup grape tomatoes, halved
  • 2.0 whole eggs
  • 0.25 cup ham, chopped

Instructions

  1. Take a skillet and place it over medium heat
  2. Add butter and wait until the butter melts
  3. Add onion and bell pepper and Sauté for a few minutes
  4. Take a bowl and whip eggs
  5. dd the remaining ingredients and stir
  6. Add Sautéed onion and pepper, stir
  7. Microwave the egg mix for 1 minute
  8. Serve hot!
