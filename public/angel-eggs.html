Angel Eggs

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 30.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 eggs, hardboiled and peeled
  • 1.0 tablespoon vanilla bean sweetener, sugar free
  • 2.0 tablespoons Keto-Friendly mayonnaise
  • 0.125 teaspoon cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Halve the boiled eggs and scoop out the yolk
  2. Place in a bowl
  3. Add egg whites on a plate
  4. Add sweetener, cinnamon, mayo to the egg yolks and mash them well
  5. Transfer yolk mix to white halves
  6. Serve and enjoy!
