Asian Glazed Salmon and Cauliflower

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 boneless fillets of salmon
  • 2.0 cups (around 550 grams) of frozen cauliflower rice (or freshly ground cauliflower rice using a food processor)
  • 4.0 tbsp of liquid aminos or soy sauce
  • 2.0 tbsp of shallots, chopped finely
  • 2.0 tbsp of sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Make a marinade of the liquid aminos, shallots, and sesame oil and combine them all into a bowl.
  2. Soak the salmon fillets to the marinade and optionally refrigerate for at least an hour before cooking.
  3. Pop these into the oven and bake for 10-12 minutes.
  4. While the salmon cooks, heat and prepare the frozen cauliflower rice, according to package instructions.
  5. Serve the salmon over the cauliflower rice hot

Nutritional Info

  • Calories: 210.0 kcal
  • Fat: 13.0g
  • Carbs: 4.0g
  • Protein: 15.0g
