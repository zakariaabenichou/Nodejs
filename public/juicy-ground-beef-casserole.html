Juicy Ground Beef Casserole

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 35.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 teaspoons onion flakes
  • 1.0 tablespoon glutten-free worcestershire sauce
  • 2.0 pounds ground beef
  • 2.0 garlic clove, peeled and minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 cup mozarella cheese, shredded and russian dressing
  • 2.0 cups cheddar cheese,shredded
  • 2.0 tablespons sesame seeds, toasted
  • 20.0 dill pickle slices
  • 1.0 romain lettuce head, torn

Instructions

  1. Take a pan and place it over medium heat
  2. Add beef, onion flakes, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper and garlic
  3. Stir for 5 minutes
  4. Transfer to baking dish and add a 1 cup of cheddar, mozzarella cheese, half of dressing
  5. Stir and spread evenly
  6. Arrange pickle slices on top
  7. Sprinkle remaining cheddar and sesame seeds
  8. Transfer to oven and bake for 20 minutes at 350-degree F
  9. Turn oven to broil and broil for 5 minutes
  10. Divide lettuce between serving platters and top with remaining dressing. Enjoy!
