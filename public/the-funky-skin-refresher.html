The Funky Skin Refresher

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 cup ice
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach, chopped
  • 0.5 cucumber, diced

  • 0.5 lime, juiced
  • 0.5 lemon, juiced
  • 1.0 cup of water

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
