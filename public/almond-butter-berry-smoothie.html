Almond Butter Berry Smoothie

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 cups kale, chopped
  • 2.0 cups almond milk, unsweetened
  • 1.0 cup grapes, mixed
  • 1.0 banana, peeled and frozen
  • 4.0 tablespoons almond butter, raw
  • 1.0 tablespoon chia

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add the vegetable/fruits
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
