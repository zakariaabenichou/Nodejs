Delicious Turkey Stew

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 25.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 12.0

Ingredients

  • 15.0 ounces can eat vegetables, mixed
  • 2.0 cups turkey, cooked and cubed
  • 14.0 ounces can chicken broth
  • 3.0 tablespoon butter

Instructions

  1. Take a saucepan and put all the ingredients into it.
  2. Bring it to a boil.
  3. Reduce the heat to low.
  4. Simmer for about 25 minutes.
  5. Dish out and serve hot.
