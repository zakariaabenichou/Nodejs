Sensational Lemonade Fat Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 120.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 a lemon
  • 4.0 ounces cream cheese
  • 2.0 ounces butter
  • Salt to taste
  • 2.0 teaspoon natural sweetener

Instructions

  1. Take a fin grater and zest lemon
  2. Squeeze lemon juice into bowl with zest
  3. Add butter, cream cheese in a bowl and add zest, juice, salt, sweetener
  4. Mix well using a hand mixer until smooth
  5. Spoon mixture into molds and let them freeze for 2 hours
  6. Serve and enjoy!
