Parmesan Pork Steak

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 15.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.5 pound pork steak
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1.0 ounce parmesan, grated/melted
  • 1.0 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2.0 tablespoons olive oil

Instructions

  1. Beat the pork steak with kitchen mallet to flatten it a bit
  2. Season with salt and pepper
  3. Let it rest for a few minutes
  4. Take a frying pan and grease it with oil, place it over high heat
  5. Once the oil is hot, add steak and cook for 7-8 minutes per side
  6. Transfer cooked steak on a plate and drizzle lemon juice
  7. Cover with grated parmesan/melted parmesan
  8. Serve and enjoy!

  • Calories: 315.0 kcal
  • Fat: 27.0g
