Cheesed Up Bacon Butternut Squash

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1.5 pound sliced butternut squash
  • Kosher salt & Black pepper
  • 1.5 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2.0 chopped bacon

Instructions

  1. Set the oven to 4250F to preheat, then grease the baking tray
  2. Add the olive oil in a medium skillet to heat to sauté the bacon, butternut squash, and the seasonings for 2 minutes.
  3. After 2 minutes, pour everything on the baking tray to bake for 25 minutes
  4. Remove from the oven, sprinkle the parmesan cheese on top the bake for 10 more minutes
  5. Serve the meal while still warm.
