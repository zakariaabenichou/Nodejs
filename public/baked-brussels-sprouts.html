Baked Brussels Sprouts

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 20.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 1.0 tablespoon avocado oil
  • 2.0 garlic cloves, minced
  • pinch of salt and black peppers/4 cup cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. In a roasting pan, combine the sprouts with the oil and the other ingredients, toss and bake at 400 degrees F for 20 minutes.
  2. Divide everything between plates and serve.
