A Green Bean Mixture

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 120.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 cups green beans, trimmed
  • 2.0 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1.0 tablespoon date paste
  • Salt and pepper as needed
  • 0.25 teaspoon coconut aminos

Instructions

  1. Add green beans, date paste, pepper, salt, coconut aminos, and stir
  2. Toss and place lid
  3. Cook on LOW for 2 hours
  4. Serve and enjoy!

  • Calories: 236.0 kcal
  • Fat: 6.0g
  • Carbs: 10.0g
  • Protein: 6.0g
