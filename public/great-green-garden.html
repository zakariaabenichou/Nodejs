Great Green Garden

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 teaspoon spirulina
  • Few fresh mint leaves
  • 0.5 cup cucumber, peeled
  • 0.75 cup plain coconut yogurt
  • 1.0 cup pineapple, frozen
  • 1.0 cup mango, frozen
  • 1.0 cup unsweetened coconut milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first

  5. Add a few ice cubes and serve the smoothie
  6. Enjoy!
