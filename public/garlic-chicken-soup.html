Garlic Chicken Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons butter
  • 4.0 ounces cream cheese, cubed
  • 14.5 ounce chicken broth
  • 2.0 cups chicken, shredded
  • 0.25 cup heavy cream
  • 2.0 tablespoons Stacey Hawkins Garlic Gusto Seasoning
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Take a saucepan and place it over medium heat.
  2. Add butter into the saucepan and melt the butter.
  3. Put the shredded chicken to the pan and coat with melted butter.
  4. Add cream cheese and Stacey Hawkins garlic gusto seasoning when chicken is warm
  5. Mix to blend the ingredients.
  6. Add chicken broth, heavy cream, and evenly distributed cream cheese.
  7. Bring them to boil, then reduce the heat to low
  8. Simmer for 3-4 minutes.
  9. Add salt to taste and serve.
  10. Enjoy!
