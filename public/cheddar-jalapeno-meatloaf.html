Cheddar Jalapeno Meatloaf

Category: Beef Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 40.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 lb ground beef
  • 0.5 tsp cumin
  • 2.0 jalapenos, sliced
  • 1.5 tbsp of garlic salt
  • 1.5 cups of cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375F/180C.
  2. Combine all the ingredients together except the jalapenos and cheese.
  3. Fill a deep baking dish (around 8X8 inches) with the ground beef and spice mixture, top with the jalapenos, and finish with the layer of cheddar cheese.
  4. Bake in the oven for 35-40 minutes. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving and cut ideally into squares or triangles before serving
