Spicy Fired Up Jalapeno Poppers

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 30.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 5.0 oz cream cheese
  • 0.25 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 8.0 medium jalapeno peppers
  • 0.5 tsp Mrs. Dash Table Blend
  • 8.0 slices bacon

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F/200°C.
  2. Cut the jalapenos in half.
  3. Use a spoon to scrape out the insides of the peppers.
  4. In a bowl, add together the cream cheese, mozzarella cheese, and spices of your choice.
  5. Pack the cream cheese mixture into the jalapenos and place the peppers on top.
  6. Wrap each pepper in 1 slice of bacon, starting from the bottom and working up.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes. Broil for an additional 3 minutes.
  8. Serve!
