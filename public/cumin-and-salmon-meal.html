Cumin And Salmon Meal

Category: Fish and Seafood Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 salmon fillets, boneless
  • 1.0 tablespoon avocado oil
  • 1.0 red onion, sliced
  • 1.0 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1.0 teaspoon cumin, ground

Instructions

  1. Heat up a pan with the oil over medium-high heat, add the onion and chili powder and cook for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the fish, salt, pepper, and cumin, cook for 4 minutes on each side divide between plates and serve.
