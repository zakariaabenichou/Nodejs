Terrific Jalapeno Bacon Bombs

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 10.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 large jalapeno peppers
  • 16.0 bacon strips
  • 6.0 ounce of full fat cream cheese
  • 2.0 teaspoon of garlic powder
  • 1.0 teaspoon of chili powder

Instructions

  1. Pre-heat your oven to 350-degree Fahrenheit
  2. Place a wire rack over a roasting pan and keep it on the side
  3. Make a slit lengthways across jalapeno pepper and scrape out the seeds, discard them
  4. Place a nonstick skillet over high heat and add half of your bacon strip, cook until crispy; Drain them
  5. Chop the cooked bacon strips and transfer to large bowl; Add cream cheese and mix
  6. Season the cream cheese and bacon mix with garlic and chili powder; Mix well
  7. Stuff the mix into the jalapeno peppers with and wrap raw bacon strip all around
  8. Arrange the stuffed wrapped jalapeno on prepare wire rack
  9. Roast for 10 minutes
  10. Transfer to cooling rack and serve!
