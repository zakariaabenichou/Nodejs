Worthy Bacon-Wrapped Drumsticks

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 480.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 6.0

Ingredients

  • 12.0 chicken drumsticks
  • 12.0 slices thin-cut bacon

Instructions

  1. Wrap each chicken drumsticks in bacon
  2. Place drumsticks in your Slow Cooker
  3. Place lid and cook on LOW for 8 hours
  4. Serve and enjoy!
  5. Tip: If you don’t have a slow cooker, you may use an Iron-Cast Dutch Oven. The temperature is 200 Degrees F for LOW and 250 degrees F for HIGH

  • Calories: 202.0 kcal
  • Fat: 8.0g
  • Carbs: 3.0g
  • Protein: 30.0g
