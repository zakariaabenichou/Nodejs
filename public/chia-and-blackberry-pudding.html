Chia And Blackberry Pudding

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 45.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 0.25 cup chia seeds
  • 0.5 cup blackberries, fresh
  • 1.0 teaspoon liquid sweetener
  • 1.0 cup coconut milk, full fat and unsweetened
  • 1.0 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Take the vanilla ,liquid sweetener and coconut milk and add to blender
  2. Process until thick
  3. Add in blackberries and process until smooth
  4. Divide the mixture between cups and chill for 30 minutes
  5. Serve and enjoy!
