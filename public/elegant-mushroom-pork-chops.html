Elegant Mushroom Pork Chops

Category: Pork And Other Red Meat

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 40.0 mins –

Difficulty: Meduim | Servings: 3.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  • 1.0 teaspoon garlic
  • 1.0 onion, peeled and chopped
  • 1.0 cup Keto-Friendly Mayonnaise
  • 3.0 pork chops, boneless
  • 1.0 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1.0 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 0.5 cup coconut oil

Instructions

  1. Take a pan and place it over medium heat
  2. Add oil and let it heat up
  3. Add mushrooms, onions and stir
  4. Cook for 4 minutes
  5. Add pork chops, season with nutmeg, garlic powder and brown both sides
  6. Transfer the pan in oven and bake for 30 minutes at 350-degree F
  7. Transfer pork chops to plates and keep it warm
  8. Take a pan and place it over medium-heat
  9. Add vinegar, mayonnaise over mushroom mix and stir or a few minutes
  10. Drizzle sauce over pork chops; Enjoy!
