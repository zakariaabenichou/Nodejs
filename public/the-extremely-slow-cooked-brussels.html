The Extremely Slow Cooked Brussels

Category: Vegan and Vegetarian Recipes

Prep Time: 15.0 mins –

Cook Time: 240.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 1.0 pound Brussels sprouts, bottom trimmed and cut
  • 1.0 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1.5 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 0.25 cup water
  • 0.5 teaspoon dried tarragon

Instructions

  1. Add Brussels, salt, water, pepper, mustard to Slow Cooker
  2. Add dried tarragon and stir
  3. Place lid and cook on LOW for 5 hours until the Brussels are tender
  4. Stir well and add Dijon over Brussel
  5. Stir and enjoy!
  6. Tip: If you don’t have a slow cooker, you may use an Iron-Cast Dutch Oven. The temperature is 200 Degrees F for LOW and 250 degrees F for HIGH
