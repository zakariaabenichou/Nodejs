Spinach And Bacon Salad

Category: Appetizer and Snacks Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 5.0 mins –

Difficulty: Medium | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 oz spinach
  • 4.0 sliced of bacon, chopped
  • 2.0 eggs, boiled, sliced
  • 0.5 a cup mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Take a skillet pan, place it over medium heat, add bacon, and cook for 5 minutes until browned.
  2. Meanwhile, take a salad bowl, add spinach
  3. in it, top with bacon and eggs and drizzle with mayonnaise.
  4. Toss until well mixed and then serve.
