Mango Energizer

Category: Drinks And Smoothies

Prep Time: 5.0 mins –

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 2.0

Ingredients

  • 4.0 tablespoon protein powder
  • 2.0 teaspoons spirulina
  • 1.0 teaspoon bee pollen
  • 1.0 frozen banana, sliced
  • 1.0 cup of frozen mango, sliced
  • 2.0 cups baby spinach
  • 1.25 cup unsweetened almond milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients except vegetables/fruits first
