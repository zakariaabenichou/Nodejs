Delicious Breakfast Sausage Casserole

Category: Breakfast Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins –

Cook Time: 45.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 8.0 eggs, beaten
  • 1.0 head chopped cauliflower
  • 1.0 lb sausage, cooked and crumbled
  • 2.0 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 1.0 cup sharp cheddar cheese, grated

Instructions

  1. Cook the sausage as usual.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the sausage, heavy whipping cream, chopped cauliflower, cheese, and eggs.
  3. Pour into a greased casserole dish.
  4. Cook for 45 minutes at 350°F/175°C, or until firm.
  5. Top with cheese and serve.

  • Calories: 290.0 kcal
  • Fat: 25.0g
  • Carbs: 1.0g
