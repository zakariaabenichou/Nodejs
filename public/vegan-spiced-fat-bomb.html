Vegan Spiced Fat Bomb

Category: Desserts Recipes

Prep Time: 10.0 mins – + 30 min chill time

Cook Time: 0.0 mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 0.75 c of pumpkin puree
  • 0.25 c of hemp seeds
  • 0.5 c of coconut oil
  • 2.0 tsp of pumpkin pie spice
  • 1.0 tsp of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Take a blender and add all of the ingredients
  2. Blend them well and portion the mixture out into silicon molds
  3. Allow them to chill and enjoy!
