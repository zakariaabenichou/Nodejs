Healthy Cucumber Soup

Category: Soups and Stews Recipes

Prep Time: 14.0 mins –

Cook Time: mins –

Difficulty: Easy | Servings: 4.0

Ingredients

  • 2.0 tablespoons garlic, minced
  • 4.0 cups english cucumbers, peeled and diced
  • 0.5 cup onions, diced
  • 1.0 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1.5 cups vegetable broth
  • 0.5 teaspoon salt
  • 0.25 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 0.25 cup parsley, diced
  • 0.5 cup Greek yogurt, plain

Instructions

  1. Add the listed ingredients to blender and emulsify by blend them (except ½ cup of chopped cucumbers)
